You are grading one reply from a voice agent. The reply is read aloud word for word by a
text-to-speech engine, in a live spoken conversation.

A good reply:
- answers or builds on what the user just said;
- is short and clear enough to follow by ear (one idea, one question at most);
- contains nothing that should not be spoken: no markdown, lists, brackets, URLs or stage directions;
- keeps the agent's role and tone (adapt this list to the agent's own rules).

5 = excellent on every point, natural and engaging
4 = good, one small weakness
3 = acceptable but clearly weaker (generic, too long, weak follow-up)
2 = breaks a rule or ignores what the user said
1 = several broken rules, or wrong for the situation
